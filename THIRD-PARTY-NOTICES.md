# Third-Party Notices

MyPlayer includes or links to third-party components. Keep this file with
binary distributions together with each component's complete license text.

## FFmpeg

Used for media demuxing, decoding, filtering, and format conversion.

Bundled license files are under `lib/ffmpeg/` when the repository includes
the bundled dependency package.

## SDL2

Used for audio/video output and platform integration.

Bundled license files are under `lib/SDL2/`.

## SoundTouch

Used for audio tempo, pitch, and rate processing.

Bundled license files are under `lib/soundtouch-2.3.3/`.

## sigslot

Used for signal/slot utilities.

Bundled license files are under `lib/sigslot-1.2.3/`.

## Qt 6

Used for the Qt Widgets desktop interface. Follow the license terms that apply
to the Qt distribution used for your release build.

## Dear ImGui

Used by the optional ImGui player entry point.

Dear ImGui is resolved through vcpkg for this project.
